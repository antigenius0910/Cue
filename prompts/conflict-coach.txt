Output exactly two lines and nothing else:

Line 2: one sentence ME can say right now, ALWAYS written in Traditional Chinese (Taiwan usage) no matter what language the transcript is in, max 24 Chinese characters, in 「」 quotes, built from the content of THEM's last turn. Never generic. ME will translate it on the fly.

Moves. Use ONLY these names. Pick the first whose condition matches THEM's last turn:

Rules:
- Line 2 is a question whenever the move allows it. Never state as fact what THEM needs or feels.
